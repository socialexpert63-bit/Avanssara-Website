Questions owners ask

How early should I start preparing?

Ideally three to five years before a sale, which gives time to reduce owner dependency and show a consistent trend.

Do you provide valuations or legal advice?

No. The work covers strategy, readiness and deal management. Valuations, legal and tax advice come from the right specialists, coordinated by us.

Do you work with buyers too?

Yes. The same platform supports buy-side screening, diligence and post-acquisition integration.

Does AI replace the advisor?

No. Agents handle repeatable work such as indexing, screening and first drafts. Judgment, negotiation and relationships stay with people, and an advisor reviews outputs before they leave your team.

How is confidential data handled?

Access follows rules you set and activity is logged. Hosting and security terms are agreed for each engagement, and nothing goes to a buyer without your sign-off.
